# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive and harassment-free research
environment. Be constructive, distinguish evidence from opinion, respect
privacy and dataset licences, and avoid overstating medical or scientific
claims.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, disclosure of
private information, deliberate research-integrity violations, and publishing
credentials or restricted data.

Report concerns privately to the repository owner. Maintainers may edit,
remove or reject contributions and restrict participation when necessary to
protect contributors, participants, data subjects and research integrity.
